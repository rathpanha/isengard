# Contributing

Isengard is open source under the GNU GPL v3 (`LICENSE`). Forks and copies are fine.

**Pull requests and other contributions are not accepted.** Please do not open PRs expecting them to be merged. Bug reports and discussion may still be useful; there is no commitment to act on them, so an issue may not get picked up or fixed. Use the [bug report form](https://github.com/rathpanha/isengard/issues/new/choose).

Third-party projects we build on are listed in [docs/credits.md](docs/credits.md).
